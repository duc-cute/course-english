Can you explain the difference between 'affect' and 'effect'?

auto_awesome

Certainly! This is a common point of confusion.

  • 'Affect' is usually a verb (to influence or produce a change).
  • 'Effect' is usually a noun (the result or consequence of an action).

Examples:
"The weather affects my mood" (Verb)
"The medicine had a positive effect" (Noun)
